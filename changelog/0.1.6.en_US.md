# Mouse 0.1.6: open source

Mouse is now open source, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, at github.com/brenoxavier/SteinMouse.

- **Open source**: anyone can read the code, report a bug pointing at the exact line and contribute.
- **A third-party mod**: Mouse now comes from its own repository instead of the Stein Client's official list. If you already have it, the Stein Client keeps updating it automatically. Nothing changes in how it works, and your settings stay where they are.
