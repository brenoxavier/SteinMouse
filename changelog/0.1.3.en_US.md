# Mouse: clicks only in the right window

Nothing changes on screen: the gestures are the same and clicks still go out one per tick.

- **Fix**: a click still waiting in the queue (from the wheel or from Shift-dragging) can no longer land in another window — if a chest closes and another opens right after, whatever was left of the gesture is dropped.
- **Under the hood**: the mod no longer talks to Minecraft directly — keys, chat, screens, players and inventory go through Stein Loader.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.23**: update with the installer before the mod.
